Fixes a crash at launch on macOS 26. Version 1.0.12 wouldn't open on Macs running macOS 26. This update opens normally again. Thanks to everyone who reported it.

New
- Accessory groups: see the groups you made in the Home app with "Group with Other Accessories", create new ones, rename them, and add or remove accessories.
- Each gang of a multi-gang switch can be renamed on its own, and a switch or outlet can be set to display as a light or fan.

Fixes
- HomeClaw no longer quits without warning when a command-line client disconnects before reading its reply.
- Reading an accessory that reports an unlimited range, such as an Eve Energy outlet, no longer freezes every later request until HomeClaw restarts.
